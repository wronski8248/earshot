    Natural AI voice

    Kokoro is an open-source AI voice that runs on your phone. It sounds more natural than the built-in voices, and it keeps reading when your screen is off.

    • One-time download. After that it works offline.
    • It prepares audio ahead of where you're listening. Wait until a few minutes are ready before you lock your phone.
    • It uses more battery while it prepares audio.

    Fast mode uses your phone's graphics chip, so it prepares audio several times faster, but it's a bigger download. If it doesn't start on your device, Earshot switches to the standard download.
